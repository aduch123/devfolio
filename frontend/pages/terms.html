Using Devfolio

Devfolio helps developers create and share portfolios. Keep your account credentials private and use the service responsibly.

Your content

You retain ownership of your portfolio content. Publish only material you have the right to share. Public portfolios can be viewed and indexed by others; anyone holding a private share link can use it until it expires or is revoked.

Respectful use

Do not use portfolios, messages or links for harassment, spam, fraud or unlawful activity. Do not attempt to access another person's account or interfere with the service.

Your choices

You can edit or unpublish your portfolio, revoke private links and request account deletion through Settings. Unpublishing cannot remove copies already made by visitors or search engines.

Availability

Keep your own copies of important content. Analytics and health suggestions are estimates, and the service does not guarantee employment opportunities or uninterrupted availability.
