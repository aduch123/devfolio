Privacy

Devfolio uses first-party analytics to help portfolio owners understand visits and link clicks. Analytics records include a hashed identifier, referrer hostname, device category and timestamp. We do not store raw IP addresses or full user-agent strings in the analytics database, use analytics cookies, or send analytics to third-party trackers.

Visit identifiers rotate daily and differ between portfolios. A separate persistent hash prevents repeated anonymous likes; shared networks and device changes can affect these counts. Raw visit records older than 13 months are rolled into daily totals.

Contact forms send your name, email address and message to the portfolio owner and may use Resend for email notifications. Account sessions use essential authentication cookies. Operational server and hosting logs may contain network identifiers for security and troubleshooting.

Portfolio owners can delete messages and manage their accounts. This notice must be completed with the operator's contact details and applicable retention/legal information before public launch.
